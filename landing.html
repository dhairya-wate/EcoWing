Platform Architecture

Built for Autonomous Environmental Monitoring

Engineered from sensor to cloud to provide conservation authorities and municipal cleanup teams with verifiable aerial intelligence.

Onboard Neural Vision

Edge neural networks categorize waste into 8 environmental classifications with high-confidence bounding boxes in under 80 milliseconds.

Autonomous Mission Planner

Generate grid-survey flight paths over rivers and shorelines with automated terrain-following, geofence barriers, and intelligent Return-to-Home.

MongoDB Geospatial Store

Every detection is indexed in MongoDB with 2dsphere GeoJSON coordinates, enabling historical trend queries, density heatmaps, and CSV/GeoJSON exports.

Multi-Drone Swarm Control

Synchronize up to 10 autonomous airframes over Socket.io WebSockets with central pilot handoff, emergency override, and battery burn monitoring.

Fail-Safe Security Core

Hardened command links with AES-256 telemetry encryption, low-battery auto-return, and instantaneous one-touch Emergency Stop capabilities.

Site Analysis & Analytics

Real-time Chart.js statistical pipelines visualizing detection confidence, hourly class shares, and hot-zone contamination distribution.
